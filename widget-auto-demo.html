Riverton Hawks coach Dana Ortiz said Thursday that guard Marcus Bell is questionable for Friday night's season opener against the Lakeview Wolves after he rolled his right ankle in practice earlier this week.

Bell, who averaged 22.4 points and 6.1 assists last season, did not take part in the team's full-contact drills Wednesday and Thursday. Ortiz said the swelling has gone down but that the team will wait until pregame warmups to make a decision.

"He's a competitor and he wants to play, but we're not going to rush him back for game one," Ortiz told reporters after practice. "We'll see how he moves tomorrow and go from there."

The Hawks, who finished 31-21 last season and lost in the second round of the playoffs, would turn to second-year guard Theo Grant if Bell sits. Grant started 11 games last year and shot 41 percent from three-point range in a reserve role.

The opener tips off at 7:30 p.m. Friday. The team will release its official injury report by 5 p.m., and Ortiz said he expects to know more after Bell's shootaround.
