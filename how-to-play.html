How to play Marqit

Marqit is a free daily prediction game. Every day you make three Yes/No calls on trending sports, pop culture and news. Everyone around the world gets the same three calls.

The three steps

  1. 1. PickAnswer Yes or No on the 3 daily calls. Once you pick, the crowd split appears and you can comment.
  2. 2. LockEach call locks before its event starts. At lock your call is set and can’t be changed.
  3. 3. ScoreYou earn 100 points for every correct call, added after the event.

Play with others

Streaks

Play every day to build a streak. Go 3 for 3 for a Perfect Day. One free Streak Freeze covers a missed day. You earn badges at 7, 30 and 100 days.

Buddies

Add a buddy from the Play tab. When you both make the same pick and it’s correct, you each get +25. Opposite picks earn no bonus.

Rivals

Add a rival from the Play tab. You each start with 5 golden stars. Whoever gets more of the day’s calls right takes a star from the other. A tie changes nothing. A 5-day streak earns an extra star.

Good to know

Is Marqit free?
Yes. Marqit is free to play.
Is money involved?
Playing is free and there is no wagering or cash prizes. Marqit+ is an optional paid subscription.
What if I miss a day?
Your streak resets unless you have a Streak Freeze. Your points stay.
What about ties?
A tied Rival day changes nothing. No stars move.
Who can play?
You need to confirm you are 18 or older when you sign up.
