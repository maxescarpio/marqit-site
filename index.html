Privacy & restrictions.

Privacy Policy

We collect your email, a username, your self-reported state, and confirmation that you're 18 or older when you sign up.

We track which market categories you engage with — sports, crypto, culture, and so on — so we can personalize which notifications we send you. We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

Push notifications require your explicit permission through your browser or device. You can opt out of personalized notifications at any time.
