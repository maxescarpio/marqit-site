About Marqit.

Marqit is a free daily prediction game. Every day you get three questions — one from sports, one from pop culture, one from the news — and you call each one Yes or No before the day's events play out. Everyone gets the same three questions, at the same time, worldwide. That's the whole idea.

We built Marqit because opinions are more fun when they're on the record. Calling something and finding out you were right — or confidently wrong — is a small, satisfying ritual, the same way a daily word puzzle is. We think that applies just as well to the calls you'd make anyway while watching a game or scrolling the news.

There's no cost to play and no way to spend real money inside Marqit — no purchases, no wagering, no cash prizes. Points, streaks, and tiers exist purely for bragging rights: something to track, compare with friends, and build over time.

Privacy Policy

We collect your email, a username, your self-reported state, and confirmation that you're 18 or older when you sign up.

We use your results on the daily calls to run streaks, leaderboards, and reminders. We don't sell your personal data to third parties.

Your username, streak, points, and tier are visible to other users on leaderboards. If you set a buddy or join a group, your results on shared questions become visible to that buddy or group as part of how Buddy Bonus, Rival Bonus, and group leaderboards work.

Push notifications require your explicit permission through your browser or device. You can opt out of personalized notifications at any time.

Terms & Conditions

  • You must be 18 or older to use Marqit.
  • Marqit is free to play. No purchase is ever necessary to participate or win.
  • Points and in-app tokens have no cash value and cannot be redeemed, transferred, or exchanged for money or prizes.
  • Marqit is not gambling and does not involve wagering for real-world value.
  • One account per person. Availability may vary by state.
  • Group invite links are not access-controlled beyond the code itself — anyone with the code can join. Don't share it publicly if you want to keep your group private.
  • You may belong to one group at a time. Setting a buddy is one-directional and doesn't require their confirmation.
