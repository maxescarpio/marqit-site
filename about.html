About Marqit

Marqit (also written playmarqit) is a free daily prediction game. Every day you make three Yes/No calls on trending sports, pop culture and news, and everyone around the world gets the same three.

What is Marqit?

Pick Yes or No on each call, lock it in before the event starts, and earn points when you are right. Build streaks, add buddies and rivals, and climb five tiers from Rookie to Legend. You must be 18 or older to play. See the full rules and scoring.

How to find us

The game lives at playmarqit.com. Searching for Marqit game or playmarqit will bring you here. Every finished day is saved in the daily results archive.
