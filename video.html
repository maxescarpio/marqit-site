Marqit Video Guide

Street interviews and 40–60 second recaps. Internal, please don’t share.

Make a video

  1. Open the Video Maker on your phone.
  2. Choose your video.
  3. Captions start by themselves. The first time takes a minute or two.
  4. Fix any wrong words. Two people talking? Tap the colored button to switch speaker.
  5. Tap Export video, keep the screen on until it finishes, then tap Save to Photos.
Open Video Maker

Filming tips

  • Film vertical.
  • Prop the phone up so it doesn’t shake, or use the front camera.
  • Stand somewhere quiet with light on your face.
  • Keep your face and chest in the middle of the frame. The captions sit there.
  • Recap: one person talks straight to the camera, 40–60 seconds.

Rules

  • Interviews: under 90 seconds. Recaps: 40–60 seconds.
  • Only the MARQIT watermark and the captions on screen.
  • Auto captions not working? Use Type captions instead.
