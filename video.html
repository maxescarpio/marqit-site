Marqit Video Guide

Street interviews and 40–60 second recaps. Made in CapCut on desktop. Internal, please don’t share.

Step 1: Get the watermark

Download watermark

Step 2: Set up CapCut (once)

  1. New project. Set ratio to 9:16.
  2. Import the watermark and a video. Put the video on the bottom track and the watermark above it. Stretch the watermark to the end of the video.
  3. Text → Auto captions → Generate.
  4. Click the captions → Animation → Captions → pick a free style where words pop up one at a time.
  5. Rename the project TEMPLATE.

Step 3: Every new video

  1. Duplicate the TEMPLATE and open the copy.
  2. Swap in the new video. Stretch the watermark to match.
  3. Generate captions again. Two speakers? Give each a different color.
  4. Export.

Out of free captions? Ask Max.

Rules

  • Interviews: under 90 seconds. Recaps: 40–60 seconds.
  • Only the MARQIT watermark and the captions on screen.
