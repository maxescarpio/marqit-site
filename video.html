Marqit Video Guide

Street interviews and 40–60 second recaps. Made in CapCut (desktop). Internal, please don’t share.

1. Download the watermark

Download watermark See safe zones

It’s see-through, 1080×1920. Don’t resize or move it.

2. Build the project once

  1. CapCut → New project → under Modify, set ratio to 9:16.
  2. Import the watermark and your video.
  3. Drag the video onto the bottom track. Drag the watermark onto the track above it.
  4. Drag the right edge of the watermark so it ends exactly where the video ends.
  5. Text → Auto captions → Generate (language: English).
  6. Click the caption bar → Animation → Captions → pick a free style (no purple diamond) that shows words one at a time.
  7. Click the caption bar → Text tab → color yellow. Keep captions out of the bottom 20% of the screen.
  8. Rename the project Marqit Interview TEMPLATE (three dots on the project → Rename).

3. Every new video

  1. Three dots on the TEMPLATE → Duplicate. Open the copy, never the template.
  2. Delete the old video and the old captions.
  3. Import the new video onto the bottom track. Stretch the watermark to match.
  4. Text → Auto captions → Generate, then re-pick the caption style if it reset.
  5. Two speakers? Click each speaker’s caption bars and set a different color for each.
  6. Export. Save as MP4, 1080p, 30fps.

Free CapCut limits auto captions. When “uses left” hits 0, ask Max.

Rules

LengthInterviews: under 90s. Recaps: 40–60s. Instagram Reels and TikTok both. On screenOnly the MARQIT watermark and the word-by-word captions. Nothing else. SpeakersOne caption color per person. Safe zoneKeep text out of the top 170px, bottom 420px and right 140px, where the apps cover it.
