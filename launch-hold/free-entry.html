Free entry by mail

No purchase necessary. Free entrants and Marqit+ members have the same chance.

Each week Marqit gives away a prize. Marqit+ members are entered in each weekly drawing automatically. Anyone else can enter the same drawing for free by mail, whether or not you have a Marqit account. A free mail-in entry has exactly the same chance of winning as a member's entry. No purchase is necessary to enter or to win.

How to enter

  1. Write your entry by hand on a plain 3" × 5" card or a piece of paper that size. It must be your own handwriting. Printed, copied or typed entries are not accepted.
  2. Put this on the card:
    Marqit Free Entry — [DRAWING NAME / DATE]
    Full name:
    Date of birth:
    Home address:
    Email:
    Signature:
  3. Mail it in its own envelope, with your own postage, to:
    Grand Media Group LLC
    Marqit Free Entry
    [MAILING ADDRESS]
  4. Watch your email. We confirm each valid entry by email. An entry that is missing information, arrives late, or is not handwritten does not count.

The short rules

  • One entry per person per drawing, one entry per envelope. To be in a drawing you must send a new card for that drawing. Cards do not carry over to later weeks.
  • Marqit+ members are entered automatically and do not need to mail a card. Members and mail-in entrants each get one entry per drawing, with equal odds.
  • You must be 18 or older and live in [ELIGIBLE STATES / REGIONS].
  • Entries must be postmarked by the mail-by date and received by the receive-by date.
  • We do not ask for a photo of your ID. If you win, we will confirm your age and identity before the prize is sent.
  • Marqit points, streaks and tokens have no cash value.
